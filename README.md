## 👋 About me

I work at the seam between IT and OT. I come from chemical-plant
operations — Siemens PCS7, ICS/DCS — and years of watching alarms
taught me that the boring log is usually the one that matters. Now I
spend my time on the other side of that: securing industrial networks,
detection engineering, and incident response.

Currently finishing an **M.Sc. in Cybersecurity &amp; Privacy**, with an
emphasis on network security, cryptography, and industrial cybersecurity.

**Ask me about:** chemical plant operations · Siemens PCS7 · ICS/DCS ·
IT/OT interactions.

> Off-hours: Dostoevsky, trading-card market data, and a voice assistant
> I keep overengineering.
